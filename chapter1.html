REMNA PASALṬHA

By – Laltena Khiangte

INRUNNA RAPTHLAK

Khawfing chat tur intham, khawthim zual lai tak chuan a rawn khuang leh ta al al mai le. Sangul tun vel atanga ngharmut tawh Lal chhawnthanga chu rilru hlimna thuk tak avang chuan tuma hriatpui lohin a khi suk thei hial a. An arpa ken an khuan leh takah chuan midangho pawh chu an rawn thangharh tih hriat tak hian thawm an rawn nei sat sat hlawm a.

Khawfing a chat fel tihah chuan arpa chu rawn khuang leh al alin Lal Chhawnan aw an then kharh kharh tak ah chuan khawnbawl Ramngaia chu rawn tawng chhuak in khuanu malsawmna kan dawng ngei dawn in ka hria e. Kan tlang zuan hi a thiang ngeiin ka ngai an rawn tih takah chuan pasaltha Liannghaka leh tlang valho pawh chuan an rawn tuihnih liam liam a.

A ni reng lah taka. Zan thum an riahna a nih tawh a. An riah tir atang rengin an arpa hluisen ken chuan hunbi chhiar bawhpelh miah loin leng len hun atanga khuan hnuhnung thlengin pelh miah loin rawl a chhuah ziah a ni a. Upa Rama chauh hi lungawi a ni bik hauh lo. Lal chhawna pawh thawhphnan nei miah loin buk tuala rawn chhuah pahin khuanuin zah a ngai em ang a, heng baka chian leh zualna kan la mamawh dawn chuang em mawni le chawngi pa tiin upa Rama chu hlim hmel pusarh chung hian an ti zui a. Upa rama pawh chuan Lalpa kei zawng han sawi serh tur leh lungawi lohna reng ka hmu lo ve. Harsatna awm thei ber tur a lang kham huaiho lah kan pasalthaho huaisen zarah kan thuhnuai ta bawk si a. Mahse kan pasalthate hian eng rilru nge an put phei zawng va hre suh i an ti ve zat bawk a.

Pasaltha Liannghaka chu an hotu deuh ber leh phanchang ber a nih bawk avangin leh Lalpa leh khawnbawl upa chuan beisei nei taka an rawn en dun reng bawk avanga rawn tawng chhuak vein kei ni teh lulin eng va hre suh i. Mahse min han ngaihchan der takah chuan a mual inkhawh dan leh tlang bulthut atanga mual inchawi chhoh dan reng reng atang hian khan awm dan turte, sa leh ral laka pawh tanhmun a tha dawn hlein ka hria. Kan aienna ber pawhin a thiang a ti bawk a tih pahin an arpa chu an melh zauh a. Lalpa leh upa te inlungawi chuan kei nizawngin khawiah pawh kan zui ang che u an ti a. Tlangval ho pawh chuan an pawmpui zia lantir nan an lu an lo bu nghauh nghauh bawk a.

Lal Chhawna chu a pasaltha Liannghaka tawngkam tawi fel tak chuan a ti lawm hle mai. Tuisen lui an zawh tan atang renga tuna an awm mek na tlang in chawi chhoh dan leh tlak lam zawng kham rang chawi phei zui mai leh chhim leh hmar lam tlang dung inkhawh phei thui tak mai tawpah tlang dung hriam tak lehlam lehlam a awi bungvut mai a ni a. Chhuah chhawng lam hi a mual inkhawh a rem em em mai a. Tlang dung thui tak leh zau tha tak mai phul hmun niin Lal mual tur awm renga duan sa ang mai a mual rem leh pharh tha tak mai te chuan saipum khaw lal leh a thuihruai te khaw thar sat tura kalte lung a dum tak meuh a ni. A ni ve lah taka, tuna an awmna hi hnawhsarum taka an inbenbelna kha a ni a. Chhuah lamah chuan a thengthaw tha viau nain tlak lamah erawh khamrang bak hmuh theih a ni lo.

Lal chhawna chuan kan lung a dam tlang a nih zawngin tukthuan ei khamah kir zai kan rel mai em ni ang tiin a khawnbawl leh a thuihhruaiho chu an rawn hlawm a. An rilrem zawng tak a ni tih lan tir nan an lo tuihnih tliar tliar a. Chutihlai mek chuan tlangvalho huan Lal mit tlun tum takin rawng an lo bawl mawlh mawlh bawk a. Pasaltha tuai tir Lalthangliana zukla kah sa rep an la ngah bawk a eirawngbawl pawh harsa lo tak a ni.

Tlangvalte an phet sauh sauh lai chuan Lal Chhawna chu inthiar chhuanlamin an vak chhuak a. Rilrua ngaihtuah muk tak chuan khua an chuan vang vang a. Pawi ralho tan pawh helai rawn thleng tur chuan zan tam karkawngah an rawn riah a ngaih dan tur te a chhut neuh neuh a. A mumang mak tak mai kha ngaihtuahin tawnmang lasi ni awm takin a hruai a a hmuh tirna hmun kha he lai ngei hi a ni tih chu an thlen hlim atang khan chiang takin a hre daih tawh a. Saipum khaw Lal a lo nih tak mai dan te, nunau tam tak enkawla manganna thuk tak an paltlang lai te a rilruah chuan a lo lang zut zut mai. Thangkhat lian lai liam tawh mah nise nimin mai a thil thleng ang mai a chiang hian a mithlaah an rawn lang zut zut mai a. A rilru na chu a rum chhuak kawk thei hial a ni.

A ni lah tak a. Thangkhat lian liam taah khan Lal Sairuma khua saipum chu khaw inlungrual tak niin, an khaw tui tlan a tha bawk a thalai pawh an harhvangin tam pawh an tam ve reng a. In zathum a in chhal khua niin an in lungrualna leh an lal rorel thiam tak mai leh zaidam tak mai a vang hian tu khua mah an ngai bik lo. An ram bawh a tha bawk a thawkmawh deuh chhung pawhin kum khat kham na na na chu an thar vek mai. Buh balah an malsawmna dawn avanga boruak zang taka chapchar kut hlim taka an hman mek lai in Lal hrumcheua, pawi lal zingah pawh huaisar leh pawisak nei lo ber, lusei leh pawiho lal dang pawhin an tihkhai ber mai chuan Lal Sairuma khaw chanchin leh an thalai te chanchinte khawnuam tak leh hausa tak an ni tih lo hriain a pasaltha chhuan vawr a nunrawn em avang a an chi te zingah pawh a hming lam rik rikngawt pawh an ngam mang loh pasaltha Biakmanga hoin paaltha sawmhnih rual Saipum en thlithlai turin a rawn tir tih reng an hre lo.

Lal hrumcheua thupek ang chuan enthla tur an ni a. Saipum khaw kut hmang lah an inlungrualin ni thum ngawt mi tumah daihnai ah pawh vak chhuak awm loin an hmang a ni si a. An dai velah mi rawva tak tak pasalth Biakmanga hoin a kaiza vengtu pasaltha sawmhnih rualin an vel reng a ni tih hria se zawng zu leh sa nen, chai leh lam ringawtin hun an hmang lo tur.

Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan saipum khaw kal khan zawng zawng leh an khaw thler awmdan te, an in sak dan te atang chuan an khaw val rual dang tel lo pawhin an luh chhuah thei a ring ta tlat mai a. An in kulhna saipum hmarlam kham hnuai kawrte khawkrawk zet mai puk lian lo tak mahse thui phei raih maiah chuan puk bang nghengin mei an chhep a. Puk chung lung rawn tohlawt chuan mei eng chu a thup tha hle mai a. Kawr kam puk kawt lam erawh a rawn en raih thung.

Zanriah eikhamah chuan puk kawt mei en chin velah chuan awmkhawm taihin pasaltha Biakmanga chuan Vartianah chet lak nghal tur tiin a duhdan chu an sawi bul bul a. Tu mahin pawmpui vak lo mah se hnial ngam an awm lo. An zinga upa fapa sa pawh la ve thei tak chuan in thlahrung leh tim tak chung hian kan Lalpa khan enthlithlai tur chauhin min chah bawk si a....an rawn ti hman chauh a pasaltha Biakmanga kut atang chuan rang mangkhen hian lungtum lian tha lerlawr tak mai hi an khaw upa fapa lu zawnah chuan a rawn thlawk phei nghal fur mai. Puk mei engin a en phak a ni hlau a lung lo thlawk phei a hmuh vuai rual chuan a lo per nghal dawrh a. Chuti ni lo se chuan a lubur chu a keh chuai thei ngei ang. A ral lulak chem emaw a silai hman than em em mai a hum lai chu ni ngat se chuan khawnbawl upa fapa hian boruak hip a mamawh leh tawh ngai kher lo ang le. Tumah hian pasaltha Biakmanga hi an hnial ngam ngai lo. Hmeichhia leh naupang pawh a that hreh ngai lo tlat a ni. An lalpa pawh hian a thu hi a hnial ngai meuh lo a ni a. Kawnbawl upa fapa hian an lalpa thu hmu ni si lo, an tlem tham deuh bawk si avanga chanchhe zawk an ni thei mai ang tih a hlauhthawn avangin ti dek chungin an tawng chhuak ta kha a ni a. Tumahin a thlavang han hauh eih pawh an awm chuang lo. Pasaltha Pauliana, pasaltha Biakmanga kaiza vengtu ber chu rawn tawng chhuakin engtinnge ruahmanna kan neih ang a rawn tih takah chuan pasaltha Biakmanga chuan a ruahmanna chu an sawi dap dap a. Pawl hrang hrangah insiamin kal khan pathum atangin veng hmawrah pahnih te te a inkawpin in an rawn hal ang a. Sawmin zawlbuk an hual ang a,a bak paliin pasaltha Biakmanga hoin lal in an luh chhuah dawn a ni. Ruahmanna fel tak nen vartianah Saipum khaw phingchil tumin tlem tal chhin hman beiseiin pukah chhuan an in zui lut leh ta hlawm a.

Pasaltha Biakmanga ho te chu tum danga an chet ang chuan an phur tlang lo tih chu an mit meng atang chuan a hriat theih. Khawkhat run tur em chuan an beitham deuhin an hria a ni mahna le. Saipum khaw thalaite an chak zia leh huaisen ziate tlangkam atangin an hre ve bawk si nen a mawh lem lo e. Mahse pasaltha Biakmanga chuan an inring lo lai leh an kut hmang zu leh sa tlan a inhmang da mek te chu hup thei mai a inring a ni. An lalpa an kuta a awm theih chuan tuman chet an thawh theiin a ring lo a. A mah a inring tawk bawk nen harsa lo takin inbeihna tak tak a thlen hma in saipum khua chu an thuhnuaiah an dah theih mai a beisei a. Rosum tam tak nen an lalpa han barakhai a chak a ni. He tia tih a chakna chhan ber pawh an lalpa sal kawl lai zing a mi halkha khua an run tuma an nula hawn, ral rama an awm lai pawh a a mit it zawng tak a nih avanga insum zo loin a kakpui a kai takna nu ngei kha a hmei atan a dil chhuah a duh vang a ni a.

Leilung a lang thei tan tih velah chuan pasaltha Biakmanga hote chu an awmna tura ruatah chuan an pasaltha Pauliana leh a hoten zawlbuk lamah thawm an neih ve leh a che mai thei turin an inring thap tawh. Pasaltha Pauliana pawhin a naute ho chu tumahin a thlawnin thal thlah loh tur, tlanchhiat ai chuan tam thei angber kan thihchilh tur a ni. Tlan ka hmu a nih chuan keimah ngei hian in lu ka la ang tih chu a fuihna hnuhnung a ni. Zawlbuk chung di ro tha tak mai chu dithlifarfem hmun thum laiah chuan an han dep kai ta a. Zawlbuk tlangvalho lah chu zanlai pelh rawk thlenga chai an ni bawk a an mutui ngei mai. Zawlbuk chung zatve lai a kang kai tawh tih ah pawh tumah thawm rawn chhuah an la awm thei lo. Mei han vung chhoin thli a rawn siam tan bawk nen mei alh ri pawh a ring sawt hle mai. Chhung lamah pawh thawm a rawn awm tan ta. Tlangval pahnih in mit nuai chat chat chung leh phak tak chung a rawn tho chhuak bawhbel an rawn bawk liam chiah tihah chuan pasaltha pauliana silai chu a dawt a alh chhuak zawk khawpin a puak ta dur mai le. Silai dang rawn puak zui nghal zawtin tlangval pahnih pawh nuna nei tawh lo tih hriat takin an tlu sawp chiang hle. Silai a rik takah chuan saipum tlangvak ho chun an thil tawn chu tuma inhrilh ngai loin an chiang tlang khawp mai..

Chutihlai mek chuan pasaltha Biakmanga leh a hote pawh chu lal inah chuan an zuang lut nghal rawk a. Zawlbuk lama silai ri avanga thawhphan pawh nei hauh lo a lal Sairuma khumpui atanga zuan thawh pah a banglaia a chempui chu phawi dawk zawkin ralho lolut hmahruaitu sah chhum hmawk tuma a chem an vai fua chuan engmah a fuh ta tlat lo. A hma a mi hi pasaltha naran lo tak Biakmanga a ni tlat. Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan lal Sairuma chem rawn vai bum paha lum phei nghal zawk in a chem milu tam tak a lak tawhna ngei mai chu an vai ve nghal zawk bawk a. A tum a thelh lo tih chu lal Sairuma tre that ri chuan a ti chiang hle. Lal Sairuma ke dinglam kaiza tha chu pasaltha Biakmanga chem vai chuan a chhat hman ta tlat. Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan rang taka rawn din chhuah pahin a hote hnenah chuan that rih suh u an ti teng chhuai mai bawk a. Chutih lai chuan khumai lam atanga thawm hria a rawn tho chhuak mek chu pasaltha Biakmanga chuan zuan pahin a chem chu chhuk phei deuh zawngin an vai ngek mai bawk a. Lal fanu sangpuii a hmel that avang leh a zaidam em avanga pitar putar thlenga an ngainat em em chuan a thlarau a thlah fel der tih hriat tak hian a nghawng chu a thlep nghal hnawp mai. Lalnu Chhumpuii mangang taka tre chul chung a rawn tho mek chu pawi ralho chuan dim baksak lo hian an lo phih vak thuai mai bawk a. Lal fapa te pahnih lah zawlbuk lamah an riak ve dai bawk si a. Chhantu tur an va ngai tak em.

Lal mualah pawh chuan thawm a tha tawh kher mai. Leilung pawh a lang chiang tawh hle a. Khatia silai an puah tak thuai thuai a ral ral ti chunga lal fapa leh midang paruk chem hum chunga rawn dawk chhuak te pawh chuan lei an rawn rah hma in pawiho ngenmu chuan an hnuk a chhat fel nghal der a. Tlangval rawn harh chiang ta deuh hoin fimkhur a ngai e tia tih dan tur anhan rel leh nak nak chu pawi ralho tan silai zen sawhna hun tha an pe a ni leh ta ngawt mai. A mawh hran lo ve. Pawn lamah hian hmelma engzatin nge hual tih hriat ni suh le. Zawlbuk chung thenkhat chu a rawn kang pawp tan ta der mai bawk si. Valupa khuma chuan ring lenglawng hian saipum tlangval te u thih leh ruam khatah dam leh tlang khatah kan ti ngai a ni lawm ni. Zam hlek suh u. Hma ka hruai ang a kan hmaah eng ralmah an ding zo tur a ni lo e. Khai le tih thawt pahin bawhbel chu an zuan kan zawk mai a. Mi dang pawh chuan rawn zui nghal in pawn lam atangin pawi ralho silai pawh a rawn puak nghal thuai thuai mai reng a. A hma hruai lam chu an vir thla nghal nawk nawk a. Mahse an silai thun hman bik tawh hek lo le. Saipum tlang val huaisen rual chuan an han bawh chilh ta riap riap mai a. Thinrim inhrosa ri nen, mahni hming chhal tawk an awm bawk, thir leh thir inkhak ri karah thirin ruh leh tisa a at ri nen a ri mep mep mai. Pawi ralho pawh inring penpawn leh ralhrat tak mai an ni bawk a saipum tlangval ten tam hneh zawk viau mah se an inveng tawn thiam in an tangrual tha riau mai a. Saipum tlangval te chu thophang leh lu la kai deuh chhulh an ni hlawm bawk si a, an hek riau mai. Tlang val engemaw zat chu an tlu tawh a. Hliam tuar chiau vawng vawng ri pawh a kat ta nuk mai. An tam hneh hrim a, pawi ralho chu lal in lamah chuan an tawlh phei pah zel bawk a. Tunah chuan paruk chauh an dam ve tawh.

Veng hmawr tin atang chuan mei khu leh mei alh in hlap ri pawp pawp hriat tur a awm a. Nunau hlau thlabar te thawm nen, na tuar te ri nen. Then khat duhlai te sun vang a tap rawi rawi ri nen. Chu zing zet chu saipum khaw vanglai hmang mek tan chuan a thleng thei ngai dawn em ni tih mai tur a ni.

Khatia zawlbuk lamah silai a puah takah khan, zawlbuk ep tuikhur mual mawng a awm pasaltha zamlova leh a aihna phahpui a kawmchhak pa pasaltha chhawnthanga te chu hlauhawm a hnai tih hriain thawhphan nei map lo hian an hman than em em mai chempui leh feikibar ngei mai chu chuh nghalin pawn lama thawm chu an ngai chang nghal a.

Pasaltha zamlova chuan a kawmthlang in hnih a tang chuan kangmei a rawn chhuak a ni tih a hre thei thuai a. Dap bang kar atang chuan an kawt lama meichher eng lo chho mek chu hmuin rang tak a dawk chhuakin leihkapui atanga zuang thlain lei a thlen rual chuan meichher kengtu lamah chuan an lum nghal tawp tawp a. Silai pawh rawn puak nghal thuaiin a tum thlakna bul chiah lei chu a phhut nghal surh mai. Din chhuah pah a zau taka pen nghal pawkin an zuang leh a hmelma pain a silai paih a a chem phawrh a tum lai chuan hnaih chilhin a chem hman than em em mai engtik lai mah a bil taka awm ngai lo ngei mai chu an vai tle zawk mai a. Chu pawi pa lu chu a taksa atang chuan a lir hrang nghal tawl mai. Chutihlai chuan silai a rawn puak lek dur mai a. Mahse pawi khawih a nei ta lo. Pawi ral pakhat zawkin pasaltha zamlova kah thluk tawp tuma a rawn tin lai tak chu pasaltha chhawna chuan a feikibar ngei mai chuan a thin hnun zawn tak a tlang pawk khawpin a rawn khawh fuh avangin a silai chu hmet per hman mah se van lam hawi zawng a puakin a silai zen thun a tam tham viau tak leh zing khawvar lam arsi lang rei deuh te chu a kap fuh mah na.

Tuikhur mual mawng lam atang chuan in kang avanga thlabar au thawm a rawn chhuak nghal mai reng a. Silai ri leh pasaltha inhrosa thawm nen, na tuar thawm an rik tak nuaihah chuan a pui a nawiin khawlaiah mangang takin an rawn tlan chhuak nghal sup sup mai bawk a. Mei an alh vung deuh chuan thli rawn siam nghalin in a in hnaih hlawm bawk nen indang inman kai nghal zel si nen mu leh mal pawh nei loin mahni fate au khawm thawm nen an ri teng tung mai. Ral hmuh mai tur an awm tak loh avang chuan pasaltha zamlova leh chhawna chuan run an tawk tih hriain nunau ho tuikhur mual a mi chu an han huikhawm thuai thuai a, lal mual lamah thawm a that em avang chuan pawh phei ai chuan tiin tuikhur lamah chuan an tlanliam pui ta ringawt mai a. Saipum khaw innghahna pasaltha te awmna veng a ni bawk a tuhmah lu hlauh tuikhur mualah chuan an awm ve lo hlauh.

Saipum khua hi tlangdung pathum insut khawmna laia awm a ni a. Tlangdung insut khawmna hmar lamah hian mual awitlan in khawh chhoin kawn bul maiah hian lal in pawh a to lurh mai a ni a. Lal in hma lam tlangdung chhim zawnga in zarpharh pheiah hian zawlbuk pawh a buk vung mai bawk a. Tlang dung rem leh nuam tak a nih avangin in pawh inhnaih te te a sain khaw lawilen nuam tak a ni reng a. Mahse hei tak hi tuna an chhiattawh ti pawi zualtu a ni tlat thung. Veng hmawr atanga in an rawn hal khan rei lo teah in man kai zelin khawpum chuan a kang a ni ta mai.

Pawi ral ho lah an hruaituin a zir bawk a meichhera in deh kai pahin mi an hmuh hmasak a piang hmeichhia leh tar pawh sawi chuang loin kut an thlak zawt zawt bawk a. Pasaltha Biakmangan a hrilh angin veng hmawr atanga in hal tanin lal mual lam chu an rawn pan khawm zel a. Zawlbuk mual laili atanga thawm a nat tak deuh hnua khawlaia rawn tlan chhuakte chu an rawm kal pah hmiah hmiah zel mai si. Pavalai leh ral la bei thei deuh chem tum leh silaite nena rawn tlan chhuakho pawh kut tawpzan am taka hmang zo hlim mutui dawn tan chauh thawn thawh an ni bawk nen, an zu bel ruah zo zai te hnuhma an luah a la cham bang bawk si a. Pasaltha inring penpawn leh pawisak nei miahlo tan chuan tlan kual zawk zawk pah a nunchan zirna tawk chauh mai an ni.

Zawlbuk tlang valho pawn pasaltha pauliana hote chu bawh huk zelin pasaltha pauliana ril uai hnuang a nui lui tih hriat taka khek uarh uarh chung a thingthi tih loh chu an tlu zo tawh. Saipum tlang val zawlbuk kai chin zahnih thethaw zingah pawh sawmnga chuang chu an tlu tawh a. Engemaw zatin hliam an tuar bawk. Veng hmawr atanga rawn luh chhuaktu te pawn nuna an rawn la hmen heuh heuh hle. Zawlbuk muala rawn lut te erawh chu tlangvalho rawn tawn tlang zo loin an lu an hloh tawh. Lal fapa upa zawk Rumthangan pasaltha pauliana nui lai tawp hlen vang vang tura a nghawng a fik hmawk rual chuan silai a rawn puak thuai a. Silai rawn puahna lam an han hawi chuan lal luhka ah chuan lalpa leh lalnu chu thingthiin dim lo tak a kek dak chunga an hrawka chempuia tin chungin pasaltha Biakmanga leh a hote pali chu an han hmu a. Lal fapa Rumthanga chuan a chem ken lai chu leihah chuan a thlauh bawl mai a. Muangchangin a tlu ta riai riai a. A buala miten an han dawm a, an han en chian chuan a thlarau a lo thlah fel der tawh tih hriat tak hian chunglam mel bial kuau chungin a bek a ngenmu luhna kawphur mai atang chuan thisen lum thar thar chu a lo luang nguai nguai mai.
