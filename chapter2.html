INRUNNA RAPTHLAK

Zawlbuk tlang valho pawn pasaltha pauliana hote chu bawh huk zelin pasaltha pauliana ril uai hnuang a nui lui tih hriat taka khek uarh uarh chung a thingthi tih loh chu an tlu zo tawh. Saipum tlang val zawlbuk kai chin zahnih thethaw zingah pawh sawmnga chuang chu an tlu tawh a. Engemaw zatin hliam an tuar bawk. Veng hmawr atanga rawn luh chhuaktu te pawn nuna an rawn la hmen heuh heuh hle. Zawlbuk muala rawn lut te erawh chu tlangvalho rawn tawn tlang zo loin an lu an hloh tawh. Lal fapa upa zawk Rumthangan pasaltha pauliana nui lai tawp hlen vang vang tura a nghawng a fik hmawk rual chuan silai a rawn puak thuai a. Silai rawn puahna lam an han hawi chuan lal luhka ah chuan lalpa leh lalnu chu thingthiin dim lo tak a kek dak chunga an hrawka chempuia tin chungin pasaltha Biakmanga leh a hote pali chu an han hmu a. Lal fapa Rumthanga chuan a chem ken lai chu leihah chuan a thlauh bawl mai a. Muangchangin a tlu ta riai riai a. A buala miten an han dawm a, an han en chian chuan a thlarau a lo thlah fel der tawh tih hriat tak hian chunglam mel bial kuau chungin a bek a ngenmu luhna kawphur mai atang chuan thisen lum thar thar chu a lo luang nguai nguai mai. ++++

Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan thum chher chhuai hian saipum khua te u in lalte nuna hi ka kutah a awm, an dam in duh chuan thuawi takin awm rawh u. Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan chu chu a fin thlak ber ang a lo ti sup bawk a. Thingaihna awm ta hek lo tlangvalho chuan hreh tak leh thinrim insehruh teuh chungin an ralthuam te chu leihah an theh thla ta hlawm a. Upa thuama chuan pasaltha zamlova te hmel a la hmuh ve loh avangin beiseina tlem chu a la nei ve tawk tawk a. A mit chu an len viau thin na in a beiseite hmel reng hmuh tur a awm lo.

Chu fei chu midang fei ni loin saipum khaw innghahna ber pasaltha zamlova kut atanga rawn thlawk chhuak a ni. Khatia tuikhur mual an mite hmun him lama an hruai hnu khan tleirawl fel deuh te leh nuhuaisen deuh te hnenah awm dan tur chiang taka chahin hmanhmawh takin lal mual an rawn pan leh nghal kha a ni a. Thli awmdan thlithlai a chet hun remchang an zawn mek laiin kha tiang khawpa rawva a chetna khan pasaltha zamlova chu che chhuak lo thei loin a siam a ni. Pasaltha chhawna chuan an dinhmun ngaihtuahin chet lak hun rih lo ti mah se a insum hman tawh lo. A fei thlawk chhuak zawk man hman deuh thaw chuan a rawn dawk ve nghal zawk bawk a. Pawi ral pakhat fei lo chhuahna lam hawi a eng thil nge lo thleng hriat tuma ka deuh hau chuan a duh aia rangin pasaltha a hmachhawn ta tlat. Pasaltha Zamlova chu pawi ral la dam zawk lamah chuan dawk zawkin an lum phei tawp tawp a. Pawi ral bul a thlen dawn chuan din chhuah pah a vir zawkin a chem vai fua chuan a tum a ti hlawhtling chiang kher mai. Pawi ral lu chu a leng nawlh theih hial a. Lei a thlen hmain a thlarau chuan pialral kawngka a kik hman hial ta ve ang.

Pasaltha Zamlova chu naupang te tuar tuar lam hawi tuma an in her tur chiah chu paih ang thlawrh hian a tlu ta tlat. Lalin luka sang atang chuan pasaltha Biakmanga hote zing ami pakhat chu silai kawhmawr atanga zen alhna hnu la khu vat vat hian pasaltha zamlova lam chu hawiin a lo ding kak tau mai. Thlil thlen dan hmu vek tu pasaltha chhawna thin rim lutuk chu inhum ruh ngawt ngawtin a mah leh amah chu infuih mawlh mawlhin awmze neia chet lak a ngai. Thinrim avnga rilru fim hlauh loh tur....a in khap dai thei hram hram chu a ni.

Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan saipum tlangvalho chu hnangin a hnungkhirh tir ta vek mai a. Lal tualah chuan an dahkhawm hul mai. Upa ho chu vin thak thak pah hian thi leh dar, ro in neih ang ang rawn la khawm rawh u tih leh lalin a lalnu leh lalpa hruailuh chu a rual ta nghal a. Saipum khawmite la inthiarfihlim thei chin chu dai lamah an liam zo tawh. Khawlai chu ruang leh thisen in a kawi nuaih a ni ber mai. Thetn khat laiah mei a la vung that viau laiin lal mual lamah chuan in kang bang mut khu rum rum chuan khawlai pawh a ti lang fiah thei lo. Lal luhka ah chuan pasaltha Biakmangahote zing a mi pahnih chu silai nen inring takin hmun tin chu an hawi u luk hle a. Saipum tlangvalho thinrim chuan tawngkam phawk phawhraw tak takin an rawn au ve reng bawk a. Nakinah chuan lal in chhung atang chuan aw ngaihnobeilo zet hian an hrawk va chhat vek rawh u a rawn ti pawr chuai ta mai. Pasaltha Biakmanga thinin saipum tlangvalho tawng a tuar ta lo. Mal leh mala inbeih a huai zawk zawn tih velin hmun a chang ve lo.

Pasaltha Biakmanga tawng hre ve phaka awm pasaltha Chhawna chuan chet lak vat a hun tih hriain a rilru a buai hle mai. Pasaltha Biakmangahote pahnih lah chu rawva taka kaihhruai than tih takah phur takin lal tualah chuan chempui phawrin an chhuk nghal a. An mahni hnai tu a piang chu nuihsawh chungin an hrawk an zai sak ta hmiah hmiah mai le. Saipum tlangvalho thinlung na leh engmah tithei silote chan chu a va chhe teh reng em. In hrosa thawm leh anchhia in lawh bawrh bawrh thawm pawh a na ngang mai.

Pawiral pakhatin te thawt ri hria a a thianpa lam an hawi chuan a thian pa chu a nghawngah feikibarin a lo thil tlang pawk mai leh a thingthi hnawk chu a rawn hmu nghal a. Chumi rual a mit sirin thil phe vuai a hmuh chu a thil hmuh hnuhnung ber a ni ta.

Pasaltha chhawna chuan tlangvalho la damte phuarna chu an chhah sak zung zung a. Zawi deuh sapin thawm dang chhuah mai lo turin an inhrilh zel bawk a. Tlangval sawmruk lai chu an la awm. An ralthuamte an han chhar leh a siali nen an han infam ve leh meuh chuan an thinrim sosang a rei tawh bawk a insum a har a ni ang. Lal sumhmun atanga enge thil thkeng tih rawn en tum a pawi pasaltha rawn dak chhuak chiah chu a chal atanga silaimu sa takin a tukhum lamah thisen leh thluak inpawlh nuaiha a hnawl chhuah rual chuan Laikapa siali ken chu a puak thuai mai. Saipum tlangval zingah chuan Laikapa hi silai chelekah chuan tu man an khan lo. A themthiam a ni satlaih lo a. A tuan a tha em em bawk a ni. Tlangvalho chuan luhchhuah nghal mai an duh. An thawm pawh a na ngang mai. Pasaltha chhawna chuan Lalnu leh Lalpa an la kawl tlat avang chuan han bawh huk a tha berin a hre si lo. Lalin chu an han hual a, rorel vak ngaihna pawh an hre mai bik lo.

Pasaltha Chhawna chuan ring lenglawng hian lalnu leh lalpa kha rawn chhuah u la, kan nun ang che u an ti a. Chhanna a awm mai lo. Pasaltha Biakmanga pawn an chan chu a chau ta hle tih a hrethiam khawp mai. Dama chhuah dan tur ngaihtuahin ruahmanna chi hrang hrang an siam chhin thin. A hote zinga la dam awm chhun Tukvela hmelah chuan beidawna bak hmuh tur a awm tawh lo. Tukvela chuan an lalte kan pek chuan min that tho tho ang a ti a. Chu chu pasaltha Biakmanga pawn a ngaihdan chiah a ni. A beisei ang a an khua thingdim luh chu a hnaih loh hmel ta hle.

Pasaltha Chhawna chuan inphen nan an lalte an rawn hman a ring a. An kal tir a nih pawn an kal san dawn a an thah tho chu a rin dan a ni. A nauteho zinga a rin deuh deuh Laikapa, Laiawrha leh Pahlirate chu ko khawmin heng pawi mawng balte hi nungin kan vangkhua hi an chhuah san tur a ni lo. Kan lalte phawah hmangin tawlh liam an rawn tum ngei ang a, remchang in lo zawng tur a ni. Thal inthlah a nih erawh chuan ka pawh chhawn lovin an hnuk chhat nghal thei ngei tur ah chauh chet lak tur. Lal mualah hanin phenna mai tur in a awm tawh loh avangin tuallai remchang awm deuhah chuan hliampui tuar ang a awm turin an awmna tur theuh chu an kawhhmuh nghal zu g zung a.

Pasaltha Biakmanga chuan lalnu chu vin tuarh tuarhin lal thi leh ro hlu dang te chu a lak khawm tir ve mek bawk a. Lalnu lah a chhip atanga thisen lo luangin a hmai leh nghawng zawng zawng chu a bual vek tawh bawk a.

Zing ni rawn chhuak pawh chuan saipum chhiatna a tawrhpui ni ve awm hrimin a sen rum mai a. Loh theih lohna avanga rawn chhuak ni awma mawiin meikhu karah chuan duhawmna reng a chhuah lo. Engmah tih theih vak awm lo a lalin pawn leh chhunga mite an inep lai chuan pasaltha zamlova chu a rawn thaw kawk a, inbeng kan tuma an che chu pasaltha chhawna chuan a lo hmu vat a. Hmanhmawh taka tlanin a bulah chuan an thingthi nghal hnawk a. Thei leh thei lo a rawn nuih sak pah hian pasaltha zamlova chuan engemaw han sawi a tum a. Mahse a ka atang chuan thi sen rawn chhuakin a khuh zui bawrh bawrh mai. Rawn meng leh hram hramin unaupa ka nupui fanau te i kutah ka hlan e...a bik takin ka fapa Thangliana i lo kai hruai tha dawn nia. Thlahtute awmnaah ka kal tawh ang e. Ka lo thlir reng thin dawn che u nia tiin pasaltha zamlova, sa leh ral hma a zam ngailo, huaiho phei chuan an hlauh tlawn avanga amah leh a thlahte thlenga tibuai ngailo tura chhia an chham hnan, Saipum khaw muantu ber, lusei lal dangte khuaah pawh a huaisen zia leh tlawmngaih zia thang hat khawp, hringmite khawvel piah lam huaiho mai nilo lasite vangkhua thlenga hming chher tham pasaltha chuan kir leh tawhlo turin pialral lamtluang a zawh zui ve ta.

Pasaltha chhawna thinrim inhumruh lutuk chunga rum chhuak hawk chuan unaupa, ka thianpa i thihna hi a thlawn lovang. Phuba ka rawn la mawlh ang tih ka intiam e tih sap sap pahin an ding chhuak a. A thianpa thu sawi chu an ngaihtuah chiang a. Ka fapa Thangliana a ti chu khawi a mi nge ni ta ang. Tete a tanga in kawp hawt an ni a. Tuna a nupui bakah fa a neih a hre si lo. Mahse fapa pekin a awm dawn tih hriattir a ni tlat. A hming te pawh hrilh nghalin a pa hnungzui a midikte thlamuanna leh chanhaite thlamuantu, diklohna leh sual tin rengte ti tawmimtu pasaltha Thangliana chu a nupui hian a hrin sak dawn a ni. Lasite thil tih theihna bik hmangin pasaltha zamlova thisen atang ngeia lo chuak tur a ni dawn a ni. Hetiang zawng zawng hre velo chung chuan mak tih loh rual a ni lo reng a. Pasaltha Chhawna pawhin a thianpa chuan mipa fa a neih loh avanga rilru hrehawm luat avangin a hnuk chat tur chalchangin fapa neiah a inngai ta mai a nih a ring ta ber a. An nu pi makpui hi a lo ruak lo a ni ang a han ti dawn se, nizan maiah khan kut tawp zan hmang an thalaite zanlai pelh rawk thlenga an chai mup mup lai a lal mual sira an ti ti zawng zawng te kha. Fapa a awh ve zia te, kum nga chhung ringawt mai fapa beiseia an nu kakpui zantin a lawn thin dan te, tun thlenga malsawmna an dawn theih si lo zia te an sawi a an nuih pui bawk bawk kha a ni si a .

Pasaltha chhawna chuan khawnbawl upa Hratthanga chu an bekbawrh thuai a. Lalin tualah chuan an ding khawm taih a. Thil thleng chu an mit ngeiin hmu vek mah se tuman an awih mai thei lo. Lal thlazar hnuaia rorel thiam tak mah nise an boruak tawnin a zirlo em a, tun ang dinhmunah hi chuan remhriat mai bakah huaisen leh paukhauh a ngai tel tlat a ni. Ruang leh thisen luang zuih mai leh natuar rum thawm ngaihnobeilo zet zet hriat tur awm reng bawk nen, mihuaisen thisen a tang lo chuan thluakfim a chhuak thei lo reng a ni.

Pasaltha chhawna chuan tlangvalho chu hlawm hrang hrangah thenin an mi leh sa tlan darhte au khawm turin an tir darh ta nghal a. Tlangval sawm vel bawr chuan hliam ngaihvenin khawlai an fang bawk a. Hmeichhia leh rawltharho pawh awlbik awm lova tlai ni tlak dawn thleng a an buai ngar ngar hnu chuan ruang zatel tehmeuh pawh an phum zo thei ta hram a. Pasaltha Chhawna rawtna angin pawi hmelmaho lu chu lalin bulah langsar takin an tar tlar dul mai thung a. Hliam an zawn khawm zing atang pawhin a tam zawk chuan an hliam an tuar chhuak zo ta chuang lo. Tlai thim thut thutah an han phum leh rih bawk a. He ni zet hi zawng Saipum tan chuan lo her chhuak lo mai se an ti vawng vawng ngei ang. Lalinah chuan nuhoin rawng an bawl a. An han inchhiar chian chuan tlangval sawmruk, pavalai sawm tha erhawrh, nuho leh tar thlengin za chuang fe leh nunau leh tleirawl lam tihtham fe chu an la dam a, hliam enkawl ngai sawmhnih dawn an nei bawk a. Mawhphurtu ber nih huphurhawm tak a ni ta. An hmabak hi a ko em lah tak a. An khawhmun ngai a bul han tan that leh chu tuman an chak tawh lo.

Zanriah an han kil zawh chuan khawnbawl upa pahnih la damte rawtna ang chuan pasaltha Chhawna chu an khaw mawhphurhna la tur chuan an ngen ta ngawt mai a. Lal nih duhna aiin an dinhmun hian mi huaisen leh remhria a mamawh tlat a ni. Pasaltha chhawna an han tahawh mai pawh a awm tho mai. An khaw vanglai takah pawh an thlamunpui ber te zing a mi, sa leh ral lakah pawh a remruatna an hlawhtlinpui ber thin a ni reng a. Khawnbawl upate pawn khaw khat lal nih chu an chak ve ngei ang. Duh nise lalah inpuangin lal thutthleng luah pawh nise khaw mipuite chuan an hnial lo ngei ang le.

Kawnbawl upa pahnih hi midang hriat lohin an lo inrawn diam tawh zawk a ni a. Lal nihna hun tha chu an hmu thiam na meuh mai. Mahse lal dinhmun duhawmna ringawt ai chuan an khaw mite hmangaihna leh duhsakna chuan a buk rit zawk tlat a ni. An mahni anin hrechianga, dawizep vanga tlanchhia ni hauh loin an dinhmun hian remhre zawk leh chak zawk, mi huaisen leh paukhauh, nunau te pawn an thlamuanpui ngei tur hruaitu an mamawh tih an hai lo tlat a ni. Lal nih chakna lam aiin indintharlehna turah hruaitu tha an mamawh tih hriain pasaltha Chhawna chu an lal a tan an rawt ta ringawt mai a ni a. Mipui lam pawn rilru takin an thlawp a ni tih a hriat bawk a. An upa te pahnih pawh an ngaisangin an zah phah sawt zawk a ni. Mi zah phur tak chungin an dam chhung hun an hman zui pha bawk a.
